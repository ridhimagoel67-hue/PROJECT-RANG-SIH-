# Research & References

## Consumer Survey Findings

A consumer survey was run to validate demand for banana fibre / banana pulp products. Key findings:

- Most respondents reported being **confident to very confident** using banana fibre or banana pulp products for daily-use applications.
- The largest group of respondents said they would pay **up to 10% more** for banana-based sustainable products compared to conventional alternatives, with a further segment willing to pay 10–20% more or more than 20% if quality is assured.
- The large majority of respondents said they were **likely to very likely** to purchase banana fibre or banana pulp products regularly if satisfied with quality, sustainability, and price.
- A strong majority said that knowing a product is made from **agricultural waste (banana fibre/pulp)** positively influences their purchase decision, either strongly or somewhat.
